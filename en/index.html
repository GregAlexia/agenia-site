FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need an “AI strategy” before we start?

No. We start with an audit that identifies your priority use cases. You leave with a clear roadmap, even with no AI skills in-house.

Is our data safe?

Security sits at the centre of how we work. Solutions are deployed inside your own environment, with strict access control and full GDPR compliance.

How long before we see results?

A first working prototype is usually delivered in two to six weeks, which lets you confirm the value early, before any large-scale rollout.

Do you work with small companies?

Yes. Our iterative approach is built for small and mid-sized companies: start small, on one high-ROI case, then widen.

What happens once we go live?

We handle monitoring, maintenance and continuous improvement, and we train your teams so adoption lasts.

Do you work in English?

Yes. Meetings, documentation and support are available in English. The software interfaces are currently in French, and we localise them on request as part of a project.
